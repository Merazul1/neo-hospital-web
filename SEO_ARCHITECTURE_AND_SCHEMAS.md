## 1. Information Architecture & Localized Sitemap

### 1.1 Architectural Philosophy: Dual-Intent Funnel
Medical websites must serve two fundamentally distinct user behaviors:
1. **Acute High-Stress Emergency Users:** Require immediate 1-tap phone dials, ICU/NICU ventilator bed availability, real-time GPS navigation, and ambulance dispatch within seconds.
2. **Planned Elective & Chronic Care Users:** Require oncologist second opinions, joint replacement consultation, laparoscopic surgeon credentials, cashless TPA pre-authorization, and OPD timings.
